## What this says, and what follows

- The deployed cost model's coefficients describe the live table on this path. Per the pre-registration, the model's shape check (`classifyDeployedShape`, which today accepts the builder's default seven-rule policy and rejects the live six-rule table) may now be changed to recognise the live table explicitly, keeping the coefficients, with a test. **That code change is not made by this commit**; it is the next step, taken on this evidence.
- Until that change is deployed, A8 stays `unverifiable` for the live policy and the bridge's startup log says so.
- The capacity target of 6,000 pps declared on the live bridge is about 74% of the 8,140 pps mean capacity at k = 8 (the envelope's full depth), which leaves the margin below the roughly 77% bound derived from the session loss seen at 1.3 to 2.3 times capacity in the 2026-10-04 ladder.

## Deviations and incidents

1. **The first attempt was invalid and is kept** in `run-2026-10-31-live-policy-failed-attempt-1/` (same harness commit). Every step delivered about 1 packet per second against 2,000 sent (ratio 0.000 to 0.001), so every "capacity" in it is meaningless. Two preconditions that the method assumed had stopped holding, neither a property of the switch:
   - **The destination could not be resolved.** The sender sends to 10.0.0.119 (the Mac) out of `eth0`; since 2026-10-25 the Zodiac's uplink is a dead-end VLAN, nothing answers ARP, the neighbor entry on `agentic-node-01` for that address on `eth0` was `FAILED`, and the kernel never emitted the frames (`sendto` succeeded and was counted as sent).
   - **The tap NIC was not promiscuous.** The sampler reads `rx_packets` on the Jetson's `enx9c69d39dd0a6` (O2). That NIC had promiscuity 0, so it dropped unicast frames not addressed to its own MAC before counting them, and counted nothing even when frames arrived. A real capture on the same NIC showed 100 of 100 raw frames arriving.
2. **The fixes, which leave the method and the measured path unchanged.** A temporary permanent neighbor entry on `agentic-node-01` for 10.0.0.119 on `eth0` (the MAC is a locally administered one, irrelevant to the rules, which match no MAC), and a background `tcpdump -w /dev/null` on the Jetson that held O2 promiscuous for the duration. Before the rerun a 3-second burst showed the counter seeing 2,690 of 2,732 frames sent (98.5%, the same constant receiver-side offset of about 1.3% the 2026-10-20 run recorded). Afterwards the neighbor entry was deleted, the capture stopped, and O2 is back to promiscuity 0.
3. **What the 2026-10-20 run had is not known:** it presumably had a resolvable destination (the uplink was connected then) and a promiscuous O2 NIC, but neither was recorded. Its method text did not list either as a precondition; this run's does not either (the pre-registration is unchanged), so the next run must check both first.
4. The bridge was the one found (`823ce76`, 3 uncommitted files that this run neither made nor examined), unchanged during the run; the isolation status read `verified` before, and after: quarantines left 0, isolation verified. The experiment endpoints were off (404) and unused.
