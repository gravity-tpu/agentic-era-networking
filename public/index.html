Engineering Invariants

Rules enforced by tests and CI. Agents propose, deterministic checks decide, and a person approves.

Invariant 1
No suppression of errors

No @ts-ignore, no loose any, no empty catch. Errors are handled at the protocol and state boundary.

Invariant 2
Integer accounting

Packet, byte and rate counters are 64-bit integers. Timestamps are monotonic nanoseconds. No floating point in accounting.

Invariant 3
Fail closed

Ambiguous or unrecognized packets, rules or tenant context are dropped or quarantined, never forwarded.

Invariant 4
Verify before actuating

Nothing an agent proposes reaches the data plane without passing deterministic checks and a human gate.

Invariant 5
Configurable, damped limits

No buried magic numbers. Adaptive loops are damped so they cannot flap, and are exercised on real traffic.

Invariant 6
Provenance

Telemetry without physical or cryptographic provenance is not shown as live. Disconnected hardware reports zero rates and an offline status.
